PatchMap

PatchMap is an iOS app developed by a team of four Boston College students for Hackachusetts 2026, using Kiro as the main AI development agent. The app uses a phone's gyroscope to detect potential potholes during driving sessions, relying on repeated reports from multiple users to confirm their locations. I contributed to the project's brainstorming process and helped optimize the app for different scenarios.

BC Esport Website

The Esports Club of Boston College brings gamers together to discuss their favorite games, participate in tournaments, and build connections. To help expand the club's online presence, I created a prototype website featuring introductions to executive board members and templates for future events.
